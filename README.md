## 🧭 About Me
I'm an undergraduate researcher passionate about the **mathematical foundations of deep learning** — particularly **information theory**, **optimization**, and **model quantization**.  
My academic path bridges **Computer Science** and **Applied Statistics**, aiming to understand AI systems through both theoretical and empirical lenses.

At present, I’m pursuing research directions inspired by my coursework *Introduction to Mathematics for Deep Learning* and my work in **AI Systems**, **Reinforcement Learning**, and **Distributed Inference**.
